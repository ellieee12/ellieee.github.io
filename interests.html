Other activities

Here are some activities that I enjoy doing in my free time:

Music

I have been trained in classical piano since the age of five and always enjoyed challenging myself with new experiences. I completed music exams up to Grade 8 and received a theory scholarship as the top scorer in my music theory exam. In secondary school, I played the trombone in the school's marching brass band. The discipline and dedication required in music have shaped who I am today. And now, I am enjoying learning the guitar for fun.

Hiking

I also enjoy hiking, as it allows me to connect with nature and clear my busy mind. Moreover, the breathtaking views from the top make every challenging step worthwhile. I've explored the Calanques near Cassis and the Pink Granite Coast in Bretagne. These experiences have taught me patience, perseverance and the value of enjoying the journey as much as the destination.
