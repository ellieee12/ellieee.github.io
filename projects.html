Solace Sphere Application

Feb 2021 - Apr 2021

Java

HTML

Solace Sphere is an award-winning application developed for Technovation Girls, where my team received the Asia Senior Division Regional Winner title. Disguised as a Magic8Ball game, the app creates a safe and accessible space for victims and survivors of domestic abuse in Malaysia's multicultural communities. Its key features help users identify their situation, access essential information and resources, connect with domestic violence organisations, and find support within a caring community.
