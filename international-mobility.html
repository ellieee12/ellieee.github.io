International Mobility

Since I am an international student at ENSEEIHT, it is not an obligation for me to do my international mobility. Moving to France straight out of high school to pursue my studies wasn't easy. I arrived in a country where I didn't speak the language and had to adapt to a completely new environment. Even though there were ups and downs, this opportunity, made possible through an excellence scholarship, has been an eye-opening and incredible experience. I have learned a new language and discovered new cultures through traveling in Europe. I have also met amazing people along the way which has helped me grow and mature into the person I am today.

Studying Abroad in France

I was inspired by my French teacher in secondary school, who often told us about the wonders of French culture. Fascinated by the idea of studying in France, I worked hard to earn an excellence scholarship from Telekom Malaysia, the country's largest telecommunication company, to make that dream a reality. In 2022, I spent a year learning the language and engineering subjects in the city of Tours. Although it was difficult in the beginning, I learned to adapt, stay resilient and embrace change.

Pursuing my Bachelor's and Master's in Toulouse

After a year of learning French, I felt like a fish out of water when I moved to Toulouse to pursue my bachelor's degree in computer science. However, I tried my best to practice the language and that is when I started making friends with locals. I also slowly fell in love with the charm of the South of France. Time has flown by, and I am now pursuing my master's degree at ENSEEIHT, continuing to grow both personally and professionally while exploring new opportunities to push me out of my comfort zone.

Lam Berambeh in London

I"Lam Berambeh" means "let's gather" in Sarawak Malay, and that is what me and other Sarawakians living across Europe and the UK did in London, on May 3rd 2025. Sarawak, one of the states in Malaysia, organises this annual event to bring together Sarawakians, making it possible for students and professionals alike to reconnect and celebrate our shared heritage. I had the wonderful opportunity to broaden my perspective by forming meaningful new connections. Of course, I took the chance to explore and experience the beauty of London while I was there.

Travelling around Europe and North Africa

I took the opportunity of being in Europe to travel around, notably to Italy, Portugal, Morocco, Belgium and Norway. These journeys allowed me to broaden my perspective on different cultures and ways of life. Planning and organising these trips also helped me strengthen my organisational and budgeting skills, as well as my ability to adapt and navigate new environments with confidence.
