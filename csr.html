Progress with purpose

Solving problems faced by humanity has always been at the heart of engineering, driving the advancement of technology and civilisation. However, as the consequences of careless engineering decisions become more apparent, it is increasingly important to integrate social and environmental considerations in the pursuit of progress. This is where CSR plays a vital role, ensuring that engineers and corporations alike make responsible, ethical decisions that protect the safety and well-being of everyone. As a future engineer, I recognise the importance of engaging in CSR activities, not only to contribute to the community, but also to gain awareness about certain issues that might not be prevalent in my day-to-day life.
