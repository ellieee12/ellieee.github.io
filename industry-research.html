Industry Research

Although I am still exploring my exact career path, I am certain that I want to be in the field of either information system management or software development. To better understand these fields, I reached out to engineers working in related domains to learn more about the industry and the current job market. Here is what I discovered from these role models.

The Architect Behind the Systems

I interviewed Boris Billy, an information systems architect at Akkodis with over 10 years of experience. He shared insights into the different levels of architects and the skills required, ranging from technical knowledge to commercial awareness. He also discussed the job market, career progression and the role of artificial intelligence in information system architecture.

What It Takes to Be a Full-Stack Developer

To better understand the developer job market in Malaysia, I interviewed Mohamad Suhairi MD Subhi, an ENSEEIHT alumni and full-stack developer at Telekom Malaysia. He shared insights on the challenges of keeping up with rapidly evolving technologies, the real impact of AI on software engineering, and what it's like working at Telekom Malaysia, the company that sponsors my studies. High-Stakes Data: Life as a Squarepoint Engineer

I spoke with a software engineer at Squarepoint, where he manages data pipelines for investment strategies. The role requires strong technical skills, problem-solving, and communication, as the data directly affects trading decisions. Despite the high-pressure environment, he values the company's benefits and emphasizes adaptability and independent problem-solving as essential for IT in finance.
